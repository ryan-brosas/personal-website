RYAN BROSAS BRAND RULES

IDENTITY
- Brand name: Ryan Brosas.
- Descriptor: "Agent Systems Builder." Keep it secondary to the name and removable from compact contexts.
- Primary statement: "I build agent systems so repetitive work stops coming back to you."
- Approved campaign line: "Building systems so everything doesn't need you."
- Do not attach the campaign line permanently to the logo lockup.
- Use the R/lightning symbol without redrawing or altering its geometry.

PALETTE
- Near-white #FEFEFE: primary reading canvas and light surface.
- Charcoal #1A1A1A: main text and preferred dark canvas.
- Coral #FF5555: repeatable accent for primary actions, selected states, and expressive detail.
- Signal yellow #ECC90F: scarce signal, warning, focal highlight, or logo tile.
- Do not add decorative hues or gradients. Do not use a full-page yellow field behind the yellow mark.

LOGO FILE MAPPING
- Logo---Ryan-1.svg: charcoal mark on light surfaces.
- Logo---Ryan-2.svg: coral mark on controlled light or dark surfaces.
- Logo---Ryan-3.svg: yellow mark on charcoal or very dark protected plates.
- Logo---Ryan-4.svg: white mark on charcoal, coral, or protected dark imagery.
- Prefer SVG. Raster logos are compatibility-only.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Display: "Segoe UI Variable Display", "Segoe UI", Inter, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
- Body: "Segoe UI Variable Text", "Segoe UI", Inter, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
- Mono: "Cascadia Mono", "SFMono-Regular", Consolas, monospace.
- Use normal-width sans typography with moderate weights and comfortable line height.
- Do not use condensed or narrow fonts. Use mono only for labels, metadata, and technical values.

LAYOUT
- Use an 8px spacing rhythm, 1px borders, and 0/4/8px radius roles.
- Keep product and workbench screens calm, flat, modular, and readable.
- Reserve expressive collage, oversized type, and strong crops for presentation or campaign moments.
- Use one decisive expressive flourish per screen, not several competing gestures.

VOICE
- Write in a casual, nerdy, exploratory, honest, non-corporate voice.
- Explain what Ryan is exploring, what worked, what broke, the tradeoffs, and the next question.
- Use first person, concrete verbs, plain technical language, short paragraphs, occasional fragments, and genuine questions.
- Personal build logs can use natural humor such as lol or kek; do not force it.
- Client explanations use: problem, system, tradeoff, evidence, next step.

PROCESS-FIRST RULE
- Do not assume the answer is AI. Choose honestly between an agent, a script, or a process change.
- Show context sources, checks, handoffs, human oversight, and recovery paths.

AVOID THESE PHRASES
- unlock the power of AI
- revolutionize
- 10x productivity
- digital workforce
- seamless
- cutting-edge
- transform your business
- AI-powered solution

PROHIBITED TREATMENTS
- No purple gradients, generic SaaS feature-card fields, excessive pills, decorative left-accent cards, or filler copy.
- No glowing robot brains, neon AI heads, floating chat bubbles, humanoid robots, or generic future-of-AI imagery.
- No invented clients, metrics, testimonials, outcomes, or claims.
- Never copy third-party layouts or media from Untitled.png; it is a contained reference board only.

WHEN INFORMATION IS MISSING
- Label it Verified, Proposed, or Open.
- Use an honest placeholder such as "Open - owned project image needed."
- Do not guess or fabricate the missing fact.
