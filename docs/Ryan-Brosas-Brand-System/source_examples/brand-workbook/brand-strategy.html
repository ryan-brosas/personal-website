01 / Brand Strategy

Build the system behind the task.

For founders whose business is running, but still needs them for repetitive work, checking, research, and moving information around.

Secondary track

Preference persists across pages.

01 / Premise

A practical reason to exist.

Language locked
Verified intentPurpose

Reduce recurring dependence on the founder.

Build agent systems and practical automation around repetitive work, checking, research, context, handoffs, oversight, and recovery.

VerifiedPrimary statement

I build agent systems so repetitive work stops coming back to you.

Optional supporting line: For CEOs and founders whose business is running, but still needs them for everything.

Verified
Building systems so everything doesn't need you.

Approved campaign and presentation line. Keep it separate from the permanent logo lockup.

02 / Audience

Tired founders first. Builders second.

Verified

Primary / Personal track

Overworked CEOs and founders

The business works, but recurring work and decisions still route back through them. They know automation might help; they do not know what to automate first or how deep to go into AI.

Secondary / Personal track

Operators and advanced builders

Agency owners, developers, and systems nerds who already moved beyond ordinary prompting and want to exchange ideas about context, evaluation, handoffs, and reliability.

Primary / Client track

Find the right first automation

Map recurring founder dependencies, identify whether the bottleneck is AI, scripting, or process design, and choose the smallest reliable intervention.

Proof needed

Show the operating system

Use workflows, checks, failure states, costs, terminal evidence, and handoff logic. Do not use hype, invented outcomes, or vague transformation claims.

03 / Positioning

The agent is only one part.

Verified
  1. 01 ContextWhere does the information come from, and can it be trusted?
  2. 02 WorkShould this be an agent, a script, or a process change?
  3. 03 ChecksWho or what verifies that the result is actually useful?
  4. 04 HandoffsHow do people and other agents know what happened?
  5. 05 RecoveryWhat happens when it fails at 3AM?
04 / Principles

Useful beats impressive.

Verified synthesis

Systems before spectacle.

Show context, checks, handoffs, and recovery—not only the successful task.

Useful before impressive.

Choose the smallest reliable intervention that reduces recurring dependence.

Honest about failure.

Document what broke, what remains uncertain, and where a human still matters.

Explore the whole workflow.

Follow the problem past the first prompt and into the operating system around it.

AI only when it helps.

A script or a process fix may be the better solution. Say so plainly.

Share the process.

Build in public with real evidence, tradeoffs, bugs, and useful rabbit holes.

07 / Filter

A six-question reality check.

Use before shipping
  1. Is it recognizably Ryan without the logo?
  2. Is it easy to scan, reuse, and hand off?
  3. Is the expressive gesture concentrated?
  1. Can an AI follow the rule without guessing?
  2. Does it explain tradeoffs and failure paths?
  3. Would a script or process fix be more honest?
