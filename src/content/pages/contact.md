## Bring these three things

1. **The work that repeats.** What goes in, and what good result should come out.
2. **How you handle it now.** The chats, files, tools, and people holding it together.
3. **Where it breaks.** Where facts get lost, quality drops, or the job lands back on you.

The first call is for one thing. We decide if the next step is an agent, a script, a change in process, or nothing at all.
