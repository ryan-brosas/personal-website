title: Human review
description: Human review means a person checks the work before it counts.

---

The agent does the hard part, and a person confirms the result.

Review is cheapest when it catches the few cases that matter. Sort the work so the person sees the risky parts first. Review everything and you will run out of time.

Keep a clear rubric for the reviewer. Write down what to accept and what to send back. A review with no rules turns into a guess, and guesses drift over time.
