---

Many people run the same tasks, so they can compare results in one place.

Benchmarks are useful for a broad view. They show where a model stands against others. But a benchmark task may not match your real work.

Do not lean on benchmarks alone. Test on your own data too. A model that tops a list can still fail on the exact job you need done.
