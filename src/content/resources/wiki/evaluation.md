title: Evaluation
description: Evaluation means checking how well a model or agent did the job.
pillar: agent-reliability
---

You set what a good result looks like, then measure real outputs against it.

Without evaluation you only have a guess. With it you can compare tools, track changes, and catch slips. Good evaluation uses real tasks, not just easy ones.

Evaluation is hard because the right answer is not always clear. Write down your rubric before you look at the output. A check you change after the fact is not a real check.
