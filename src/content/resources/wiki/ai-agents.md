title: AI agents
description: An AI agent is a program that chooses steps to reach a goal you give it.
pillar: ai-workflow-systems
---

It can use tools, read text, and change its plan as it goes.

A simple chatbot answers one question. An agent works over many steps. It checks its own work and tries again when something fails.

Agents are useful when a task has clear parts and a clear end. They are risky when the goal is vague or the tools can do real harm. Keep a person in the loop for work that matters.
