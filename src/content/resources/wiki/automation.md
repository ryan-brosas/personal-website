The goal is to save time and reduce dull, repeated work.

Not all work should be automated. Automate the parts that are clear, repeated, and low risk. Keep the parts that need judgment in human hands.

Start small. Automate one step, watch it, then add the next. Automation that skips the test step tends to fail in front of real users.
