title: Planning
description: Planning means an agent breaks a big job into smaller steps before it starts.
pillar: ai-workflow-systems
---

It lists what to do first, second, and third.

A plan helps the agent stay on track. When a step fails, the agent can change the plan instead of starting over. A good plan names the goal and the stop point.

Plans can be wrong. The agent may not know what it does not know. Keep plans short and check them often. A long plan made once is usually a stale plan.
