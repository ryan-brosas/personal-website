title: Guardrails
description: Guardrails are the rules and limits that keep a system safe.
pillar: agent-reliability
---

They stop bad actions before they run, or stop a run before it goes too far.

Some guardrails check the input. Some check the output. Some cap the number of steps or the cost. Each one closes one failure mode.

Guardrails are not a promise of safety. They only cover the cases you thought of. Test them the same way you test the work. A guardrail no one checked is a wall made of paper.
